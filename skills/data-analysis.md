# Data analytics

## HDB resale prices

Resale transactions from data.gov.sg, joined with MRT stations, malls, schools, and COE premiums, to see how those surroundings relate to price.

### What was explored

Distance to an MRT station, distance to a mall, nearby schools, and certificate-of-entitlement premiums, alongside the flat's own attributes. The published page walks through the joins, the plots, and the model.

### More detail

[HDB resale analysis](https://itanudjaja.github.io/hdb_v2.html)

## Retractions and journal rank

Whether a journal's retraction rate moves with its rank, not only with its impact factor.

### What was explored

Retraction notices in PubMed from 2009 to 2018, set against the 2018 Scimago Journal Rank. The retraction index is retraction notices per 1,000 articles in that window. The published page reports the correlation and the quartile comparison.

### More detail

[Retraction index vs journal metrics](https://itanudjaja.github.io/retractions.html)

## Institutional repository

Depositing an institution's articles by hand does not scale, and the version a researcher uploads is often the wrong one.

### What was explored

Open R packages to take a list of DOIs, find a legal open copy through Unpaywall, and attach the publisher's policy from Sherpa Romeo.

### More detail

[Using R to populate an institutional repository](https://itanudjaja.github.io/project.html)
